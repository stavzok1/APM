…and what changes from healthy breast to tumor — work in progress

A · Framing

The question

Three questions, one quantity — miRNA “pressure” on the 50 MSigDB hallmark programs:

  1. Which hallmark programs are most targeted by high-evidence miRNAs?
  2. Does miRNA pressure anti-correlate with hallmark target-gene expression — and is it gated by AGO/RISC?
  3. How does it vary by PAM50 / immune / stage — and what does the tumor acquire vs healthy breast?

Cohort: TCGA-BRCA primary tumors (n ≈ 1,077). Edges: miRTarBase. Programs: MSigDB Hallmark.

The objects

  • hallmark sets (50, ~4,384-gene union) · miRNA arms (~515 ranked; 721 in the landscape) · edges (miRNA→target, high-evidence flag) · AGO/RISC (rate-limiting capacity) · PAM50 / immune / stage strata.

B · Method

Step 1 — the edge map

  • Evidence tiers: reporter / protein > RNA / perturbation > binding.
  • High-evidence = ≥1 functional MTI and reporter-or-protein validation.

Step 2 — pressure (key formula #1)

\[\text{pressure}(g,s)=\sum_{m\in R(g)} \underbrace{\mathrm{sm}(m,s)\,z(m,s)\,\log_2(\mathrm{RPM}{+}1)}_{\text{expression}}\;\cdot\;\underbrace{\frac{\log(1+\text{evidence}_\text{eff})}{\text{evidence\_mass}(m)}}_{\text{evidence}}\]

  • share = the arm’s slice of gene \(g\)’s regulator budget (softmax, sums to 1).
  • z = does the arm move across tumors (the coupling signal).
  • log2(RPM+1) = anchor to real abundance.
  • spine M0 = miRTarBase-only edges, S1 scorer (softmax_z_logrpm).

6a · The master equation

\[\text{pressure}(g,s)=\sum_{m\in R(g)} c(m,g,s),\qquad c=\text{expr\_mult}\cdot\text{edge\_w}\]

  • Sum, not mean — total repressive load is additive; a gene crowded by many regulators is under more pressure.
  • \(c\) is the atom: every share, family and acquired number is built from it.
  • Everything is within-scope (cohort vs one PAM50) — subtype tables are not cohort numbers re-sliced.

6b · The expression piece

\[\text{expr\_mult}=\underbrace{\mathrm{sm}(m,s)}_{\text{competition}}\cdot\underbrace{z(m,s)}_{\text{dynamics}}\cdot\underbrace{\log_2(\mathrm{RPM}{+}1)}_{\text{abundance}}\]

  • share — arms compete for finite RISC on a gene → softmax over \(R(g)\), centered on each arm’s cohort median.
  • z — the dynamics term coupling reads; by design a stably-high arm → \(z\approx0\) → zeroed.
  • log2(RPM+1) — keeps absolute level, so a share between two near-zero arms ≠ one between two abundant arms.

6c · The evidence piece

\[\text{edge\_w}=\frac{\log(1+\text{evidence}_\text{eff})}{\text{evidence\_mass}(m)},\quad \text{evidence}_\text{eff}=\text{confidence\_logclass}+0.5\,\text{enc\_depth}\]

  • assay-directness weights: reporter 3.0 > protein 2.5 > RNA/perturb 1.5 > CLIP-binding 0.5.
  • log1p within each class → diminishing returns, defuses publication-count bias.
  • ÷ evidence_mass = promiscuity penalty — a hub can’t dominate every gene by breadth.
  • + ENCORI AGO-CLIP (α=0.5) — an orthogonal experimental layer on ~25% of pairs.

6d · Why three modes (the centering choice)

mode centering answers
softmax_z_logrpm (spine) tumor mean “who moves” → coupling
softmax_logrpm (no-z) none “who carries pressure” → attribution + cross-state
softmax_devhealthy_logrpm healthy median “what the tumor acquired”

Attribution policy: rank with global_abs_share, diagnose with global_signed_share, compute under no-z.

Step 3 — the AGO/RISC gate (key formula #2)

\[\text{gate}(s)=g_{\min}+(1-g_{\min})\,\sigma\!\big(k(\text{cap}(s)-c_0)\big)\] \[\text{gated} = \text{pressure}\times\text{gate}(s),\quad \text{gate}\in[0.5,1]\]

  • capacity = mean AGO1–4 z-score.
  • a documented sensitivity layer, not a causal model — every result reported gated and ungated.

Pressure cohort-wide — what it looks like

  • structured, not noise: a heavy tail of hub arms carries most of the load; genes have many regulators.

The two read-outs — and what each is for

Enrichment — “where do miRNAs aim?”

  • one-sided hypergeometric over-representation of high-evidence targets per hallmark, FDR across 50.
  • descriptive; uses no expression.

Coupling — “is the pressure observed?”

  • partial Spearman of pressure vs target expression on a covariate ladder (raw → +CPE/HRD → +CN → +prolif).
  • inferential; negative = repression realized.

Pipeline at a glance

C · Core results (single state)

Result 1 — the targeting map

  • miRNAs buffer signaling & plasticity (TGF-β, WNT, apoptosis, NF-κB, EMT) — not housekeeping metabolism.

Result 2 — hub miRNAs

  • broadest regulators: miR-27a, miR-125b, miR-21, miR-34a (each ~44–45/50 sets).
  • program leaders are the canonical ones: miR-34a→WNT, miR-29→EMT, miR-21/155→NF-κB, miR-17→TGF-β → internal-validity check.

Result 3 — does pressure repress?

  • 26/50 gated-negative; 21/50 survive CPE+HRD (shown).
  • positive couplings split into proliferation co-regulation (persists) vs a thinner composition artifact (collapses after purity/PAM50).

Result 4 — the subtype hub

Basal concentrates pressure on tumor-suppressor / EMT nodes — PTEN, BIM, FOXO1, CDKN1A, TGFBR2, VIM — via miR-17/20a/106b/23a/130b.

Result 5 — miRNA dosage

  • 237/756 arms marginal positive locus-CN → expression concordance; 196/756 survive CPE+HRD partial.
  • top: miR-151a at the 8q24 MYC/AGO2 amplicon; subtype hierarchy LumB≈Her2 > Basal > LumA.
  • refreshed 2026-06-24 under the S1+MIMAT spine.

D · The cross-state landscape

From one state to three

A tumor-only snapshot can’t say what’s acquired. Add GTEx-healthy → TCGA-NAT → TCGA-tumor (721 arms / 5,108 edges).

Every edge carries two measurements:

  • pressure (potential) = edge weight × abundance — cross-state-comparable (no-z).
  • coupling (realized) = ESTIMATE+epithelial-adjusted partial-Spearman, per state.

The acquired program

  • 241/721 arms gain on ≥1 axis.
  • rank dHT saturates → magnitude log2FC recovers the oncomiR surge (miR-182/183/375/93).
  • top acquired = a coherent miR-21 program.

The R / P / 0 method

Switched-on repression & released brakes

acquired_realized (610 edges) — newly switched-on; highlight miR-744-3p → HLA-G + TGFB1 (acquired immune-tolerance, candidate).

released brakes (R00, 213 edges) — healthy holds ERBB2, CXCR4, MMP2/14, NOTCH2, BIM repressed; tumor releases.

Is the de-coupling real?

  • not copy number (0/220), not promoter methylation (1/188).
  • small 4/134 direct-ATAC minority at growth/cell-cycle loci.
  • ⇒ mechanism is post-transcriptional / competitive (APA, ceRNA, RISC).

NMF — method & rationale

factor pressure matrices into co-varying programs; the menu spans aggregate, within-tumor, per-subtype, gene-pressure, signed gain/loss, and share.

factor indices renumber every rebuild → track the content factor_label.

NMF — selected results

  • programs are PAM50-divergent (Basal TNFA/NFKB, q≈1e-139).
  • but what each tumor acquires is one shared backbone (46% cohort / 68% Basal).
  • divergent baseline, convergent acquisition.

Gene-set architecture — the brake-release thesis

7,840 net-repressing vs 114 net-activating pairs: miRNAs damage engines, but their malignant effect is brake-release on suppressive programs. miR-21 #1 (93% acquired).

E · Did we believe it?

Robustness 1 — proliferation

  • adjust on MKI67 / E2F-MYC-stripped G2M / PC1 → coupling survives & strengthens (8/8 key; Basal 41/50).
  • proliferation is a suppressor confound. PTEN/CDKN1A/TGFBR2/VIM robust.

Robustness 2 — curation bias

miR-17-5p is #1 under evidence, binary and TargetScan weighting (ρ≈0.65). Not a curation artifact.

Validation 1 — protein layer (CPTAC)

Independent CPTAC-2 prospective (TMT) validates: 53/1143 genes FDR-neg; ZEB1 ρ=−0.55. Mostly mRNA-mediated.

  • A mRNA-dominant: ZEB1, CALD1, MMP2.
  • B protein/translational (the payoff): PTEN −0.10 → −0.38 via miR-17-5p, DDAH1, ARHGDIA, PEBP1.
  • C mixed: KRT8, BMP1.

Validation 2 — orphan-blind discovery

A blind scan of 20,663 orphan edges (miRTarBase-absent) vs CPTAC protein → 539 protein / 182 translational / 93 translational+TCGA-replicated candidates (76 fully uncurated).

  • method self-validates by de-novo recovery of miR-29 → collagen/ECM.
  • cell-composition confound pilot: 24 → 17 survive; robust core miR-15/16/195→PMM1, miR-19a/b→PPM1A.
  • wet-lab nominations, not validated edges.

Validation 3 — independent cohort (Buffa)

207 paired breast tumors, no TCGA overlap: partial-ρ concordance +0.32, 67% sign-concordance; 108 orphans triple-validated (TCGA·CPTAC·Buffa).

F · Wrap

Limitations

  • pressure is associative, not causal.
  • miRTarBase curation bias → read rankings, not absolute folds.
  • gene-gene topology can’t see lipid-mediated inhibition (PTEN→PIP3).
  • 164 floor0 arms lack a healthy reference; AGO gate is a bounded heuristic.
  • de-coupling mechanism not yet pinned; dosage module pre-MIMAT.

What’s next

  • stratum-resolved within-PAM50 coupling.
  • pin the de-coupling mechanism with the APM motif / SV / APA per-sample layer.
  • loss-of-repression; fitted AGO×pressure; larger per-tumor ATAC; METABRIC.

Takeaway

  1. miRNAs target signaling / plasticity programs.
  2. the malignant move across healthy→tumor is brake-release on suppressive programs — subtype-structured (Basal miR-17~92 on tumor suppressors; PTEN the through-line: core → landscape → CPTAC protein).
  3. it replicates at protein (CPTAC-2) and in an independent cohort (Buffa).

Backup — formulas

  • S1 evidence scorer (confidence_logclass weights) + ENCORI depth boost (α=0.5).
  • no-z cross-state spine; healthy-anchored dev mode.
  • hypergeometric enrichment; signed-path propagation (Katz α=0.25, 6 hops).
  • gene-role malignancy + DepMap continuous weight.
  • R/P/0 thresholds (|ρ|>0.10, q<0.05); arm-resolve Tier A/B/C.
