02 — StandingWhat the program actually established

Nine blocks. Each carries its verdict, the number behind it, and the registry row that owns it. The July–August arc was a self-falsification campaign; most of the June headlines are gone, and what remains is narrower and better founded.

The one-paragraph version

The model is a good coupling estimator and is explicitly not an attribution engine. Edge existence rests on one observational line — both copy-number instruments are retracted and a germline instrument was built and refuted. The curated edge set beats a properly-built fake by ≈0.012–0.013 ρ, a value two independent designs agree on and which shrank at every control improvement before converging. The single strongest result in the program is a negative control that correctly reports zero.

The learned model — what it is Stands

One dense learned-τ² non-negative Bayesian posterior per gene, two readouts.

π≡1 dense → coupling, attribution, identifiability; evidence-π → discovery. A half-normal slab encodes “miRNAs repress”. ν² is learned, not guessed — that, not L2, is the whole edge over the lasso, which is retired to a baseline. Engine is Gibbs, not HMC, and that was measured rather than chosen: split-half ρ 0.822 vs bagged-NNLS 0.729.

Known limits. Posterior widths are too narrow — bagged NNLS 0.745 ± 0.009, Gibbs 0.815 ± 0.084, Student-t 0.849 ± 0.051 against a calibrated band of 0.9–1.1. The Gibbs figure is imprecise, not merely different; quote it with its spread. A subtype random effect was tested and does not fix it, so the fix is reporting, not modelling. Honest widths now ship on the edge card and identifiability falls 24.8% → 19.9% [18.1–22.4]. Every internal lever lands “immaterial at n≈1000”.

MH-185 · MH-140 · MH-141 · MH-92

Edge existence One line only

Rests entirely on: curated edges anti-correlate more than abundance-matched site-free ones.

The “three independent validations” headline is false — the other two legs were both observational and never independent of each other. Three separate causal designs have now been closed:

  • CN instrument I — pi_causal = γ·b where b is an observational OLS slope, so it re-derived what it was meant to validate. Clean reduced form, arm-clustered: p=0.115. MH-124r / MH-126
  • CN instrument II — its control class was 71% real binders; against a genuinely site-free control τ=−0.0007. Language later corrected to underpowered, not refuted. MH-133 / MH-157
  • Guide/passenger within-hairpin — conceptually foreclosed and empirically an abundance confound. MH-157
  • Germline cis-eQTL MR — built at n=1,075. Genuinely exogenous, but only 10 arms reach F>10 and the reduced form is no more target-directed than for random genes (p=0.66). Germline fixes exogeneity but not exclusion. MH-159

The null is also 3–4× too narrow. Site-free pairs pass “FDR q<0.05” at 25–35%. Under an honest empirical null, 0.0% of curated edges survive per-edge. This does not say the edges are fake — a typical edge is ρ≈−0.15 against σ₀≈0.09 — it says the signal is a set-level distributional shift, not per-edge significance. Any FDR count in any doc predating this rests on the uncalibrated null. MH-123 / MH-124 / MH-154

Discovery Convergent evidence, not per-edge

Per-edge and per-family discovery in bulk are empty under an honest FDR. The deliverable is a ranked queue.

What is defensible is convergence: coupling co-varies with independent biochemistry, abundance-controlled, on multiple axes — site-level chimeric overlap partial ρ=−0.096, scanMiR KD −0.091, site-confidence rung −0.050, while site count is n.s. Quality, not quantity.

The gold set is 157 edges / 90 genes / 18 arms / 11 seed families, sha256-stamped. Always quote the 11 families beside the 157 edges: 96/157 are one family and 139/157 are the miR-17~92 polycistron and its paralogue clusters, which are co-transcribed. The honest unit is one oncogenic cluster's realized target set. The cluster enrichment survives both confounds — Manakov-conditioned OR=9.76, and abundance-quintile matched in all five quintiles — but quote the odds ratio, never the 88%, which carries HEK293T assay-coverage bias.

Do not compress this axis to “discovery is empty”. That is true only of the per-edge FDR. MH-155 / MH-156 / MH-198 / MH-199 / MH-200 / MH-207

Attribution Ranks, does not name

β ranks the canonical family above chance; it does not identify it.

On a mechanical, versioned, sha256-stamped ground truth 10× larger than its predecessor (329 genes / 92 families), raw rank is abundance 0.359 > identity 0.416 > β 0.436, all better than the 0.5 chance line. But the raw ordering is a study-bias artefact. The ground truth is defined by PMID depth, and abundant miRNAs get studied more. Holding fame constant, only β (Δ=−0.085, p=0.038) and identity (−0.077) carry gene-specific content; abundance's −0.052 is indistinguishable from zero.

The head-to-head is tied and unresolvable in principle. Resolving β vs abundance needs ~1,241 clusters = 3.8× every canonical family ever published. That closes the “scale the ground truth” item by arithmetic — do not reopen it as a scaling task.

Identity ≠ magnitude. identity is Shapley/LMG on R² with bagged-NNLS weights; the old share was argmax-β in disguise and is renamed beta_frac. They now disagree in 1 of every 4 multi-family genes, and identity can honestly say undefined (213 genes, where NNLS zeroed every family). MH-196 / MH-140 / MH-138 / MH-126

Decoy validation A bound, not a win

Real beats a matched fake by ≈−0.012 ρ, and every control improvement so far has shrunk it.

The trajectory is the single most important thing on this axis: −0.045 → −0.0306 → −0.0147 → −0.0119 as the loading handicap, the 6mer contamination, the dose imbalance and a 126× evidence hole were each fixed. Two independent designs converge on −0.012 to −0.013.

The fitted-fake caveat is now discharged. Against a fake with β refit on the site-free design — identical folds, C and estimator — β still wins on mRNA in TCGA (−0.0189, p=6.7e−09) and in Buffa (−0.0374, p=3.5e−06). It survives the subtype control (within-PAM50 −0.0154, retention 0.81), so it is not lineage-scale.

The strongest result in the program

187 genes whose curated edges are all seedless show a gap of +0.0006 — exactly zero; 810 all-seeded genes show −0.0325. A priori, sequence-only, non-circular, and the model behaves correctly on both sides. No site → no repression → no coupling → no gap. MH-136

There is no competence partition. The “27% domain” is retired: the gap scales with regulator-design width, is ~2.3–2.8× larger at n_fam ≥ 3, and is nowhere exactly zero. And width cannot be separated from abundance — spearman 0.780, both partials vanish. MH-137 / MH-139 / MH-147 / MH-169 / MH-181 / MH-206

Protein βᵗ dead Destabilisation stands

The translational latent is not supported at n=101; RPPA at n=866 says the action is transcript destabilisation.

All prior positive evidence for βᵗ was a mediator leak — fit the slope inside the fold and it vanishes. Protein carries only 4–6% of the mRNA channel's Fisher information about β. Only n fixes this, and there was never a βᵗ field.

The RPPA channel, opened for the first time at n=866 on the same patients, gives the positive result: residualising protein on its own mRNA isolates the translational channel, and rho_mrna carries the signal while rho_disc does not — flat on 849 and 897 CPTAC genes.

Do not cite a “layer split”. It was recorded and withdrawn the same day: the protein null was gene selection and power, not layer. On the same 180 genes mRNA is also n.s., both sit inside their own MDE, and paired on gene at 5× the genes the layers are indistinguishable. MH-103 / MH-203 / MH-205 / MH-114

State / progression Channel cancelled Landscape stands

Three different objects. Only two of them work.

The state channel is closed by measurement: τ is indistinguishable from zero and carries 0.6% of the information. Structurally, channel precision ∝ a² with a≈0.11, so even unlimited GTEx donors add ~1%. The additive form would have faked the headline out of attenuation. Do not rebuild it.

The abundance-level cross-state landscape is built and run — 721 arms, 5,108 edges, acquired_realized 640, lost 209. Its per-edge FDR class labels rest on the uncalibrated null and carry no banner yet.

The within-patient paired axis is the live third object, and it works where the cross-cohort channel could not: differencing against a patient's own NAT removes the baseline that composition attenuation destroys. MH-102d / MH-158 / MH-160

Outcome A well-earned negative

Pressure is exhaustively non-prognostic, and so is the learned β in aggregate.

Zero recurrence FDR across 3,368 features. The learned model closes the “you tested the weaker object” reply: per-gene yes, aggregate no, and the weights earn nothing over plain abundance. A good coupling estimator is not thereby a prognostic one.

This is credible because it is gated, not merely null — the module refuses to report unless miR-210→DRFS reproduces Buffa 2011. And Buffa is genuinely independent: Oxford, surgery 1989–92, n=207, DRFS only, not a METABRIC subset (0/207 arrays match at r>0.90). Its four reported numbers are four views of one 207-patient result, not four validations. MH-202 / MH-201 / MH-76

The paired per-patient lane Settled, mostly negative

103 matched NAT/tumour patients. Twenty rows, four positives, and one important structural lesson.

What holds. Losses do de-repress — the strongest internal control the paired design can supply, never run before, and it passes. The NAT→tumour budget relation is substantially patient-dependent: a stranger's NAT profile achieves only 24.6% of the concordance. The retention tail is ~4× the median and survives composition adjustment. And the budget construction earns its keep — but only in multi-arm families, which is why every pooled comparison said “budget ≈ level”.

What died. The “tumours converge” premise (both layers diverge). Pre-loading (fails its decoy). Per-patient identity as a hidden subset (an expression artifact). The gene-architecture gradient (a proxy for pressure variance, and conditionally it runs the other way). And retention's localisation is measurement reliability, settled by a designed orthogonal control — the important regulators are not preferentially the retained ones.

Two traps this lane paid for

“Per-patient” names two different rungs. P-across gives one value per arm/edge/gene computed across the 103 patients; P-each gives one value per patient computed across genes. They are transposes and are not interchangeable. The entire paired lane is P-across.

The site-free decoy is not a null set. Real/fake arm pairs correlate at +0.211 against a +0.124 random baseline, and decoy edges hit p<0.05 at 9.2%. REAL − DECOY always understates. It is a conservative control, never a false-positive floor.

03 — The card systemFive rungs, and the one rule that governs all of them

The cards are where the program's numbers actually live. Everything else describes them. Dimensions below were measured directly from the TSVs on 18 August 2026, not quoted from a doc.

The one rule

The rung is a property of the (card, column) pair — never of the column name. beta is edge-rung on the edge card and family-rung on the gene-family card: same name, same estimator, different unit. Four defects in a single day came from not knowing the unit, and three more came from prefix maps that matched across cards instead of within one.

Reading a value without getting it wrong

rung vs agg_of
rung is the unit a value is defined on. agg_of is the lower unit it summarises, if any — and it inherits that lower rung's caveats. A gene-rung column with agg_of=arm is one row per gene but computed by summing over arms.
domain
Row-applicability, not a description: which rows the column is defined on. A column with no domain entry is defined on every row — that is the correct default, not a gap. Measured today: no-domain columns run at a median 0.90–1.00 fill, with-domain columns 0.30–0.91.
three empty states
A blank is not a zero. There is a measured 0; there is — not scanned, where a coverage flag says the scan never covered this row; and there is — no value, scanned but empty. The flag-to-block mapping is measured, never curated.
percentiles
Always carry their denominator (p68 of 320, not of 1,959), computed over non-NaN rows only.
the four targetome universes
Get four separate axes and are never blended into one chart. Blending them is a visual act the registry forbids in words.
retention
Names two unrelated quantities: a level persisting NAT→tumour within a patient (arm rung), and a fraction of an effect surviving a covariate control (edge rung). On the cards it always means the second. The word alone never disambiguates — read the producing call.
w_max
The maximum curated evidence weight — not a β and not a dose share. top_beta_frac and concentration are the shares.
arm-rung columns
Must be constant within arm across genes. If a value varies by gene it is not an arm property, whatever its name suggests.

The gate is closed — verified today

All 702 columns across the five cards are registered in card_registry.tsv, with zero unregistered columns and zero registry entries pointing at columns that do not exist. The labels are also tested, not asserted: card_rungs.verify() checks each declared rung against the data's actual invariance given that card's key, and that is how the mislabels were caught.

The system's one real gap

There is no per-column meaning documentation anywhere. Not in the registry, not in the browser, not in the docs — it lives only in the producing modules' docstrings. domain is not it; it answers which rows, not what this is. This is the highest-value small improvement available to the card system, and it is the thing a returning reader will miss first.

06 — Open workWhat is actually left, ranked

The forward board carries more entries than this, but its own header warns that its stale-entry base rate is high — five entries were found stale in a single sweep. These are the ones that survive checking.

1 · Restore an exogenous existence validation

Named by two independent documents as the single highest-value open item in the program, and nothing else changes its standing as much. It is now tested-and-failed rather than aspirational: four designs have been closed. The live design work is the target-side seed-site instrument — a seed-disrupting 3′UTR variant perturbs the site, not arm dose, so it is blind to the confounds that killed CN. Design exists in SEED_SITE_INSTRUMENT_DESIGN.md; nothing is built and there is no registry row.

2 · Model expansion and refit

User-requested and never run. Fold the externally-validated missed edges into the HE universe and re-run the Gibbs — β is a joint per-gene posterior, so a new edge re-decomposes the whole gene and this needs a refit, not an appended weight. Priority order: the 21 gold∩external edges, then the 134 multi-evidence candidates, then the double-breast set. Some targets have zero current edges, so these are new genes, not just new regulators. Prioritise by evidence quality, not raw count. MH-166

3 · Backfill the four missing registry rows

MH-222, 225, 226, 227. Cheap, and it closes the only current break in the program's own trust order.

4 · Refresh the two summary docs

STATE_OF_PLAY.md and PROGRAM_FORWARD_BOARD.md both stop at MH-207 and are missing two whole arcs. Regenerating ARCHITECTURE.md is a one-command job that picks up ~67 findings.

5 · Document what the columns mean

702 columns, zero meaning documentation outside module docstrings. See the card section above.

Known debt, carried deliberately

  • The arm-name .N normalisation fix recovers ~18 HE arms and ~300 edges — apply at the next full refresh, not before.
  • The scrambled-seed discovery null is scoped and still unbuilt; it would not remove the arm-lane leak anyway, which is a separate open defect.
  • LANDSCAPE_REPORT.md's per-edge FDR class counts rest on the uncalibrated null and carry no banner.
  • The presentation deck's F25 figure is from 2026-06-24 and shows superseded numbers; the prose beside it was fixed in July. The deck needs re-rendering before it is shown again.
  • An arm↔target sample-set audit is deferred subproject-wide — the anti-pattern is an arm-level property computed inside a per-target loop.

Two failure modes this program keeps catching

Never headline a ratio or threshold where the mass piles up. Per-gene ceilings agreeing at correlation 0.9956 still produced 17.6% versus 25.5% on the same threshold count, because 39.8% of genes sit within ±0.01 of zero.

Most “bugs” are naming collisions. Grep the call and read the evidence column, not the status tag — a (live) marker is not evidence. The prefix-map bug produced three different symptoms before its cause was fixed rather than its instances.
